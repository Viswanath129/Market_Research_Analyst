# Startup Validation Mode

For startup ideas, evaluate:

### Problem

Is the pain real?

### Market

Is the market sufficiently attractive?

### Customer

Is there a clear ICP?

### Competition

Can the startup realistically compete?

### Differentiation

Is there a defensible reason to choose it?

### Business Model

Can it make money?

### Distribution

Can customers be acquired economically?

### Retention

Will customers continue using it?

### Defensibility

Can competitors easily copy it?

### Timing

Why now?
