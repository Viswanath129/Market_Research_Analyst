# Startup Validation Example Prompt

I have an idea for a startup: A marketplace that connects local organic farmers directly with restaurants and cafes.

Please validate this idea by analyzing the following:
- Is the pain of sourcing organic produce significant for restaurants?
- What is the realistically addressable market?
- Who are the current competitors or what alternatives exist (e.g., traditional distributors)?
- What is a viable business model for this?
- What are the major risks (operational, financial, competitive)?
- What must be validated next before building?

End with a strategic market verdict.
