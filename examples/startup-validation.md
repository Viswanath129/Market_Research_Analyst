# Startup Validation Example

Use this prompt to activate the **Startup Validation Mode** of the Market Research Analyst.

**Prompt:**

> I have a new startup idea. I want to build [insert product idea, e.g., a B2B SaaS platform for independent dentists to manage inventory]. Can you run a full startup validation analysis on this? Please evaluate the problem, market attractiveness, ICP, competition, differentiation, business model, distribution, and defensibility, ending with a Strategic Verdict.
