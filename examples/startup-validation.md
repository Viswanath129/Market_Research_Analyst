# Startup Validation

Please validate the following startup idea:

I want to build a marketplace for amateur astronomers to rent out their high-end telescopes when they are not using them.

* Who has the problem?
* How painful is it?
* How do they solve it today?
* Who already serves them?
* Why would they switch?
* Would they pay?
* How much could they pay?
* How large is the realistically addressable market?
* What makes this product different?
* Can the product reach customers?
* What could kill the business?
* What evidence is still missing?
