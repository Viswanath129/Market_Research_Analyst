# Competitor Analysis Example

Use this prompt to activate the **Competitor Research Mode** of the Market Research Analyst.

**Prompt:**

> I am researching the market for [insert market, e.g., cloud-based project management tools for creative agencies]. Can you map out the competitive landscape? Please identify Direct Competitors, Indirect Competitors, Substitutes, Incumbents, Emerging Players, and Manual Alternatives. Finally, provide a competitor comparison table evaluating Target Customer, Positioning, Pricing, Business Model, Strengths, Weaknesses, and Switching Costs.
